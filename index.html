Why melon

Every package is compiled from upstream source by melon's own build scripts, signed, and shipped as a small apk package. Nothing you don't need, everything you do.

⚡

Light and fast

musl libc, BusyBox and runit: a system that boots in seconds and stays out of your way.

KDE Plasma on Wayland

A complete, modern desktop with its own melon look, set up the moment you log in.

Apps from Flathub

Laptop ready

Install melon

About ten minutes, most of it answering the gauntlet.

  1. Download the ISO below and write it to a USB stick with Rufus, balenaEtcher, Ventoy or dd.
  2. Boot from the stick. The live session starts Plasma on its own, so you can try melon first.
  3. Keeping Windows? Shrink it first in Windows' Disk Management (right-click C: → Shrink Volume) and leave at least 20 GB unallocated. melon installs into that space and adds itself to your boot menu; GRUB lists Windows too.
  4. Double-click "Install melon" on the desktop and follow the installer. Then reboot and log in.

Survive the gauntlet

melon's installer asks you questions before it lets you in: 200 of them, shuffled every time. Wrong answers send you back. Make it through and melon installs.

Want more? Take the final trial: 50 real questions against the clock, and the last 20 must be flawless. Survivors get melon in gold (the login screen, colours and boot menu), three survivor wallpapers and a certificate that says they earned it.
